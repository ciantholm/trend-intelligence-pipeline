## Brand Detail

### Baby Phat
**Organic traffic nearly doubled over eight months with no single spike, a steady climb.**
- Likely driver (hypothesis): Sustained Gen Z rediscovery of Y2K aesthetics, not a one-time press event.
- Watch next: Confirm growth holds past summer, since sustained non-spike growth is the strongest comeback signal in this set.

### Karl Kani
**Traffic rose modestly with one clear spike in May, then partially held.**
- Likely driver (hypothesis): The Coco Jones Super Bowl LX tribute look drove a temporary but partly durable traffic lift.
- Watch next: Track whether a second cultural moment compounds this or if it fades back to baseline.

### FUBU
**Traffic collapsed 91% after an initial spike, holiday capsule hype didn't convert to lasting demand.**
- Likely driver (hypothesis): The Century 21 capsule launch drove a short press-and-nostalgia spike that didn't sustain organic interest.
- Watch next: Check whether any new retail partnership is announced before writing this off as a failed relaunch.

### Phat Farm
**Small, volatile traffic with three swings over 40%, too erratic to call a trend yet.**
- Likely driver (hypothesis): Traffic likely tracks isolated press mentions rather than sustained demand, given the size and volatility.
- Watch next: Needs more months of data before confidence can move above low.

### Rocawear
**Traffic declined steadily by 44% with no recovery spike.**
- Likely driver (hypothesis): No visible relaunch or cultural moment in this window to counter the decline.
- Watch next: Flag if a capsule or campaign is announced — currently no comeback signal.

### Cross Colours
**Traffic held essentially flat across the full period.**
- Likely driver (hypothesis): Stable, modest baseline demand with no major catalyst in either direction.
- Watch next: Low-priority for now; worth re-checking if a retail partnership surfaces.

### Brother Vellies
**Small but stable traffic, no meaningful movement in either direction.**
- Likely driver (hypothesis): Consistent niche demand rather than viral or seasonal swings.
- Watch next: Compare against a launch or press moment to see if this brand can spike at all.

### Telfar
**Traffic declined 25% from a very high base, still the largest brand in this set by far.**
- Likely driver (hypothesis): Possible post-peak cooling after a prior high-visibility period, though still dominant in absolute terms.
- Watch next: Distinguish normal fluctuation from a real downward trend with another quarter of data.

### Ami Cole
**Traffic collapsed 99.5%, consistent with the brand's announced closure.**
- Likely driver (hypothesis): Wind-down of operations and marketing ahead of the confirmed September closure.
- Watch next: Useful as a closure-detection case study — this pipeline could have flagged the drop before the news broke.

### Sean John
**Traffic is nearly non-existent and declining further from an already tiny base.**
- Likely driver (hypothesis): Domain appears largely dormant, insufficient signal to say more.
- Watch next: Confirm whether seanjohn.com is even the brand's active site before drawing conclusions.

### Freddie Estelle
**Traffic is effectively zero throughout, too thin to call a trend.**
- Likely driver (hypothesis): Cultural visibility (celebrity cosigns) isn't translating to searchable web presence, possibly due to social-first distribution.
- Watch next: Check Instagram/TikTok metrics instead of search — this brand may not be an SEO story at all.
